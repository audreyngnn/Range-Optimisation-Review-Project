Patterns and stores to watch

Patterns across categories

  • Removing a product only works if shoppers have a substitute. Coffee capsule shoppers did not switch (−$1,146/wk lost); Sunscreen and Baby food shoppers did, and both categories still grew.
  • Extra space helps, but it is not the only driver. Products that gained space usually grew, yet some grew as much with no extra space (Nestling Organics, Little Sunbeam, Gelato Lane).
  • $ growth can hide falling units. Cat food sales rose 4.7% while units fell 1.7%.
  • Seasonal categories must be judged against last year. Ice cream's +43% and Sunscreen's −52.5% are mostly weather.

Stores that lag in more than one category

  • Carindale: Ice cream and Sunscreen, both seasonal. Was the new shelf set up?
  • Toowoomba: Baby food (−$42/wk) and Cat food (−$57/wk).
  • Bondi Junction: Coffee (−$71/wk) and Ice cream.
  • Penrith: bottom of Cat food (−$62/wk), and second lowest in Baby food.

These are questions to ask the stores, not conclusions: the data has no prices, promotions or stock levels.
